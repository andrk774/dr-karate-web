# Fighter sprites

These sprite sheets replace the first version's procedural Akira and Ren fighters with Rumble and Dr. Karate, following the screenshot supplied by the user on 2026-11-20. They are newly drawn adaptations of that reference, not extracted original game sprites.

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool using transparent backgrounds. Original outputs are copied unchanged into the project; the renderer reads individual frames directly from each sheet and preserves alpha.

- `web/assets/fighters/rumble.png`: Rumble, 12 poses in a 4 × 3 grid.
- `web/assets/fighters/dr-karate.png`: Dr. Karate, 12 poses in a 4 × 3 grid.
- `web/fighters.js`: frame selection, floor alignment, facing and rendering.

Frame order: idle, low idle, walk A, walk B, punch wind-up, punch, kick wind-up, kick, jump, block, hurt, knockout. The source artwork faces right and is mirrored by the renderer for the opponent. The gameplay hitboxes and attack timing remain defined by `engine.js`.

Dr. Karate's knockout head extends just outside its nominal grid cell. Explicit atlas rectangles in `fighters.js` include the complete head and exclude that fragment from the adjacent hurt frame, without modifying either PNG. Grounded poses use individually measured alpha baselines; jump retains the standing origin so tucked feet remain raised.
